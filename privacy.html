Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Farm Buddies Autopost (“the app”) uses and why. The app is operated by Cloud Thieves Studio, the maker of the Roblox game Farm Buddies (“we”, “us”).

1. What the app is

Farm Buddies Autopost is an internal tool. We use it to upload short videos of our own game to our own TikTok account and our own YouTube channel. The app is not offered to the public: no one other than Cloud Thieves Studio can sign in with it or connect an account to it.

2. Information the app uses

From TikTok (Login Kit and Content Posting API), only after our account owner authorises the app:

  • Basic account information (user.info.basic: open ID, display name, avatar), to show which of our accounts is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos (video.upload) and to publish them (video.publish) on our connected account.

From YouTube (YouTube API Services), only after our channel owner authorises the app:

  • Permission to upload videos to our channel (youtube.upload).
  • Read-only access to our own channel (youtube.readonly): the channel name and the public view, like and comment counts of our own videos, so we can see which videos did well.

The access tokens TikTok and Google issue are stored only on our own computer in a local configuration file. They are never shared with anyone.

3. Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect, store or process personal data of other TikTok or YouTube users: no viewers, followers, comments or messages.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • This website uses no cookies, no analytics, no trackers and no third-party scripts or fonts.

4. How long information is kept

Access tokens are kept until the access is revoked or we stop using the app; then they are deleted. Videos we publish stay on TikTok and YouTube under those platforms’ own rules until we delete them there.

5. Revoking access

  • TikTok: in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions, remove “Farm Buddies Autopost”.
  • Google / YouTube: at myaccount.google.com/permissions, remove “Farm Buddies Autopost”.

6. Third-party services

The app works through TikTok and YouTube. Their handling of data is described in their own policies:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy: tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • The app uses YouTube API Services. YouTube Terms of Service: youtube.com/t/terms. Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. To deliver the pages, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Children

The app is used only by Cloud Thieves Studio and does not collect information from anyone else, including children.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we update this page and the effective date above.
